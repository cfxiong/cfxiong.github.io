<section id="research-areas" class="opportunities-section opportunities-research">
  <div class="team-section-heading">
    <p class="home-kicker">Research areas</p>
    <h2>Projects with data, policy, and real-world stakes</h2>
  </div>
  <div class="opportunities-project-grid">
    <article>
      <span>NIH-funded</span>
      <h3>Human mobility behavior and climate change</h3>
      <p>Investigating how climatic events and extreme weather influence human activities, travel behavior, and mobility patterns.</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <span>Transportation and health</span>
      <h3>Long-distance travel and disease dynamics</h3>
      <p>In collaboration with the Institute of Human Virology, this study analyzes multimodal long-distance travel and reconstructs spatial-temporal disease dynamics.</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <span>EV markets</span>
      <h3>Adoption, hesitancy, and market transitions</h3>
      <p>Examining EV adoption trends in mature and emerging markets, including Norway, Sweden, and the United States.</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <span>USDOT-funded</span>
      <h3>Carbon credits and carbon neutralization</h3>
      <p>Exploring tradable carbon and equity credits that reward sustainable travel shifts and support carbon goals.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>
