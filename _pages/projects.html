Villanova Human Mobility Labs (NovaMobility Labs)

Mobility intelligence for transportation, health, and climate.

Our group builds datasets, models, and tools with transportation agencies and health researchers. Explore four featured projects below, or visit NovaMobility Labs for our team, research platforms, and opportunities.

FHWA · Mobility data

Complete Trip Data Explorer

A linked travel dataset and interactive explorer covering approximately 28.8 million trip records across six Utah counties. Connect car, transit, and active travel to see the complete journey.

Dashboard by Ruohan Li et al.

FTA · Transit equity

Transit Equity Credits

Can tradable equity credits improve access to public transit? We study dynamic pricing and different traveler needs to explore equitable transit incentives and financing.

Led by Xin Wu and Kailun Liu.
Published in Transportation Research Part E, 2026.

NIH · Villanova × IBM Research Africa

HEAT Mobility

Satellite imagery, urban form, and socioeconomic data help us understand surface heat inequity in Johannesburg–Ekurhuleni and evaluate equitable greening scenarios.

Led by Lele Zhang.
Research under review at Sustainable Cities and Society.

NIH U54 · INFORM Africa · DS-I Africa

Human Mobility & SARS-CoV-2

Research in Nigeria and South Africa examines mobility patterns, interregional travel, and pandemic dynamics. Shared datasets and reproducible analyses support collaboration across transportation and public health.
