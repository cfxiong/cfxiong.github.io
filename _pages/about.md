<section class="home-section home-band">
  <h2>NovaMobility Lab</h2>
  <p>The Villanova Human Mobility Data Lab, also known as NovaMobility, builds transportation big-data pipelines, computational models, and applied data products using location-based services data, trajectory records, public health data, travel surveys, and transportation system measurements.</p>
  <p>Our work supports collaborations across transportation economics, built environment research, pandemic monitoring, mobility and health, safety analytics, and sustainable transportation planning.</p>
  <p><a href="/data-lab/">Learn more about NovaMobility Lab</a></p>
</section>

<section class="home-section">
  <h2>Selected Research Themes</h2>
  <ul class="home-list">
    <li><strong>Mobility big data:</strong> national and international human activity, population flow, and multimodal travel measurement.</li>
    <li><strong>Simulation and statistical modeling:</strong> agent-based mobility models, behavioral inference, and policy evaluation.</li>
    <li><strong>Transportation and public health:</strong> mobility-informed analysis of infectious disease spread and health impacts.</li>
    <li><strong>Climate and sustainability:</strong> carbon credits, EV markets, and transportation responses to climate-related disruption.</li>
  </ul>
</section>
