NovaMobility Labs

A collaborative group studying mobility, data, and transportation systems.

We are a tight-knit and ambitious research group at Villanova University. Our work connects mobility data, AI, simulation, safety, health, climate, and equity, and our culture is built around shared progress, generous mentoring, and doing good work together.
